Personalized, Predictive & Explainable
EV Management

Existing EV platforms report basic status such as battery percentage and remaining range, without anticipating future degradation or explaining why a battery is at risk. SparkEV uses empirical vehicle telemetry across four interconnected modules: evaluating driving behavior to estimate battery stress, detecting faulty charging sessions, forecasting battery health ahead of time, and predicting journey energy needs and arrival charge.

Context & Motivation

The Problem Statement

Modern EV dashboards report basic numbers such as state-of-charge (SoC), estimated remaining range, and charging status. However, these systems are passive and do not predict upcoming problems or personalize advice for the individual driver.

Limitations of Existing EV Platforms

  • Static & Reactive Monitoring: Present systems merely report current charge levels without predicting future battery condition or alerting drivers when battery degradation is accelerating.
  • One-Size-Fits-All Advice: Drivers differ substantially in their acceleration habits, daily routes, and charging frequencies; a uniform recommendation fails to meet individual needs.
  • Unexplained Warnings: Telling a driver their battery is stressed without explaining why gives them no practical way to adjust their driving habits to fix the issue.
  • Centralized Privacy Hazards: Uploading granular vehicle GPS traces, speed logs, and battery telemetry to cloud servers creates substantial user privacy risks.

The SparkEV Solution Architecture

  • Physics-Informed Battery Stress Scoring: Replaces arbitrary guesses with a battery aging model that directly connects heavy acceleration and temperatures to physical cell fatigue.
  • Long-Term Health & Acceleration Detection: Predicts battery health ten cycles into the future and automatically detects when recent wear is happening faster than normal.
  • Clear & Actionable Driver Explanations: Pinpoints the specific driving habits—such as highway cruising speed or sudden throttle bursts—that the driver can change to protect their battery.
  • Privacy-Preserving Federated Personalization: Adapts to each driver's unique habits right on their vehicle, sharing only generalized model updates without sending private trip data away.

System Capabilities

Four Core Intelligence Modules

SparkEV divides intelligent electric vehicle management into four specialized modules that work together to provide continuous driver guidance.

Module 1

Driving Behavior to Battery Stress

How it works: Analyzes driving habits in real time over 60-second driving windows to evaluate how aggressive driving physically stresses the battery. An XGBoost model calculates a Battery Stress Index (BSI) and uses Explainable AI to show which driver-controllable factors—such as excessive top speed or harsh acceleration—are causing the most wear.
Inputs: 14 driving metrics including vehicle speed, acceleration jerk, hard-acceleration and hard-braking counts, idle duration, regenerative braking share, driver aggressiveness score, ambient temperature, and route categories.
Outputs: A normalized Battery Stress Index (0–100 scale), categorized into Low (<40), Moderate (40–70), or High (≥70) stress, along with prioritized plain-English eco-driving recommendations.
Module 2

Charging-Session Anomaly Detection

How it works: Monitors electric vehicle charging sessions to identify hardware faults, abnormal voltage drops, and thermal anomalies from the driver's perspective. It uses a machine learning classifier trained on thousands of real-world charging events to flag broken chargers or unsafe battery conditions without triggering false alarms.
Inputs: 57 session characteristics derived from battery state-of-charge, terminal voltage stability, charging current curves, power delivery, cell temperatures, and total energy transferred.
Outputs: Real-time session classification (Normal vs. Faulty), fault probability score, and diagnostic indicators identifying whether the issue stems from power oscillations, voltage sags, or battery overheating.
Module 3

Battery Health & Degradation Risk

How it works: Directly forecasts battery State-of-Health (SOH) ten full cycles into the future without accumulating compounding errors. In addition, an acceleration detector compares short-term degradation against the vehicle's long-term baseline, alerting drivers immediately if their battery has started losing capacity faster than normal.
Inputs: Historical capacity drops, previous SOH measurements across past cycles, rolling degradation rates, and historical operating wear across diverse battery chemistries.
Outputs: Predicted future battery SOH percentage, classified into Low (≥85%), Moderate (80–85%), High (70–80%), or Critical (<70%) health, accompanied by a 0–100 accelerated wear warning score.
Module 4

Energy Consumption & Arrival SOC

How it works: Predicts total electricity consumption for a planned trip and provides an in-trip estimator that calculates exactly what battery percentage will remain when arriving at the destination. It continuously updates this forecast during the early stages of a journey based on real cabin heating, air conditioning, and driving speeds.
Inputs: Trip distance, vehicle auxiliary loads (cabin heating and air conditioning), outside ambient weather, speed distributions, and telemetry observed during the first 25% to 50% of an active trip.
Outputs: Expected trip energy expenditure (kWh per kilometer), total kilowatt-hours needed, and precise remaining battery percentage on arrival, preventing roadside range anxiety.

Milestone Tracking

Research & Implementation Progress

Tracking the experimental evaluation on real-world datasets and on-device microcontroller verification.

Phase 1 · Complete

Real-World Dataset Integration & Ingestion

Ingested real-world driving data from Munich (70 trips, 1,355 km), charging session records (2,649 sessions), battery cycling measurements (18,065 cycles), and cell aging datasets (842 cycles).

Phase 2 · Complete

Physics-Informed Battery Stress Indexing

Formulated a capacity-fade model connecting high temperatures and heavy charging to battery degradation, and demonstrated that driving speed and acceleration are top contributors.

Phase 3 · Complete

Direct Autoregressive Forecasting & Anomaly Detection

Forecasted battery health ten cycles ahead with high accuracy, detected early accelerated wear, and achieved 99.62% charging anomaly detection accuracy with zero false alarms.

Phase 4 · Complete

Personalized Federated Learning (FedPer)

Evaluated collaborative learning across 51 client trips, demonstrating that shared base representations combined with private local layers successfully preserve driver privacy.

Phase 5 · Complete

On-Device Embedded Microcontroller Deployment

Successfully deployed machine learning decision tree models onto an ESP32 microcontroller at 240 MHz, executing sequential inference in under 9 milliseconds with a live OLED display.

Phase 6 · Real-World Deployment

Real-World Fleet Deployment & On-Road Testing

Deploying the SparkEV edge daemon and microcontroller hardware onto operational electric campus vehicles and fleet shuttles, validating continuous real-time telemetry streaming over cellular and CAN-bus networks, testing driver responsiveness to in-cabin HMI guidance, and evaluating battery longevity in diverse real-world traffic and weather conditions.
