Context & Motivation

The Problem Statement

Modern EV platforms report state-of-charge (SoC), estimated range, and charging status, but they are largely monitoring-oriented. SparkEV addresses these challenges directly through empirical, privacy-preserving machine learning.

Limitations of Existing EV Platforms

  • Static & Reactive Monitoring: Systems do not predict future battery condition or anticipate accelerated degradation before irreversible capacity loss occurs.
  • Uniform Models Fail Heterogeneity: EV users differ widely in driving styles, route elevations, and climate conditions; a single global model provides limited user decision support.
  • Opaque Black-Box Outputs: Predictions without explanations give drivers little actionable guidance on how to adjust their speed, acceleration, or charging behavior.
  • Raw Data Privacy Risks: Streaming granular high-frequency GPS, route, and BMS telemetry to centralized cloud servers poses severe driver privacy hazards.

The SparkEV Solution Architecture

  • Semi-Empirical Physics-Informed BSI: Replaces arbitrary heuristic weights with a capacity-fade model fitted to NASA and CALCE cells, raising R² from 0.708 to 0.831.
  • Direct Autoregressive SOH Forecasting: Forecasts SOH 10 cycles ahead (R² = 0.9591) without recursive error, paired with a causal rolling rate detector for accelerated degradation.
  • Actionable TreeSHAP Attributions: Physics-informed refinement drops ambient temperature to 5th rank, elevating driver-controllable factors (speed, acceleration) to top drivers.
  • Personalized Federated Learning (FedPer): Evaluated on 51 client trips; lowers error for 32/51 established clients while FedAvg nearly matches centralized arrival-SOC (ΔR² ≤ 0.03).

Target Deliverables

Four Core Modules

SparkEV is engineered around four dedicated intelligence modules feeding a unified recommendation engine and driver dashboard.

Module 1 (M1)

Driving Behavior to Battery Stress

XGBoost regressor predicts physics-informed BSI from 14 driving telemetry & context features (R² = 0.831). SHAP reveals maximum and average speed as top drivers.

Lead: Rithanya K. A.
Module 2 (M2)

Charging Session Anomaly Detection

Trained on 2,649 real sessions (Autosun/PolyU). Weighted XGBoost detects 52/54 faults without a single false alarm (99.62% accuracy, F1 = 0.981, ROC-AUC = 0.999).

Lead: Irene Divya J.
Module 3 (M3)

SOH Forecasting & Risk Scoring

Direct Autoregressive model forecasts SOH ten cycles ahead with R² = 0.9591. Rolling rate difference detects accelerating degradation before significant loss occurs.

Lead: Shravanthi S.
Module 4 (M4)

Energy & In-Trip Arrival SOC

Stable-23 Elastic Net predicts consumption (R² = 0.897). In-trip model estimates arrival SOC from first 50% of trip with R² = 0.976 and 1.48 pp MAE.

Lead: Saravana Priyaa C. R.

Research & Implementation Progress

Tracking the experimental evaluation on real-world datasets and on-device microcontroller verification.

Phase 1 · Complete

Real-World Dataset Integration & Ingestion

Ingested TUM Munich EV telemetry (70 trips, 1,355 km), Autosun/PolyU charging records (2,649 sessions), Zenodo SOH data (18,065 cycles), and NASA/CALCE aging cells (842 cycles).

Phase 2 · Complete

Physics-Informed BSI & Model Training

Replaced heuristic weights with semi-empirical capacity-fade model ($Q_{\text{loss}} = B e^{-E_a/RT} C^m (Ah)^z$), boosting M1 $R^2$ to 0.831 and shifting SHAP attribution to driver-controllable factors.

Phase 3 · Complete

Direct AR SOH Forecasting & Fault Detection

Achieved $R^2 = 0.9591$ in SOH(t + 10) forecasting, and 99.62% charging session fault classification (F1 0.981, 0 false alarms on 530 test sessions).

Phase 4 · Complete

Personalized Federated Learning (FedPer)

Evaluated on 51 client trips. FedAvg matches centralized arrival-SOC ($R^2 = 0.973$ vs $0.976$). FedPer lowers local error for 32/51 established clients.

Phase 5 · Complete

On-Device ESP32 Microcontroller Deployment

Converted 3 XGBoost models to C headers; achieved sequential inference in 8.65 ms at 240 MHz on ESP32 with SH1106 OLED and JSON transport over Wi-Fi.
