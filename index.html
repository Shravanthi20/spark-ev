Context & Motivation

The Problem Statement

Modern electric vehicles deliver high-resolution monitoring, but remain fundamentally reactionary and static. SparkEV addresses four major structural bottlenecks in present-day EV management.

Limitations of Existing EV Platforms

  • Static & Reactive Monitoring: Present systems display current State-of-Charge (SoC) and estimated range, but fail to anticipate future battery degradation or stress points before damage occurs.
  • Ignoring User Heterogeneity: Driving styles, ambient climates, commute patterns, and charging schedules vary dramatically. Universal one-size-fits-all recommendations fail in the real world.
  • Black-Box Predictions: ML models that output a risk score without providing actionable explanations breed distrust, leaving drivers uncertain how to modify their habits.
  • Centralized Privacy Vulnerabilities: Streaming raw vehicle GPS traces, battery telemetry, and driving histories to central cloud servers creates severe privacy and security liabilities.

The SparkEV Integrated Solution

  • Personalized Federated Learning (FedPer): Each vehicle retains user-specific model personalization heads locally, while collaboratively updating shared global base representations without leaking raw driving data.
  • Temporal Battery Health Forecasting: Incorporates historical SOH and operational lag features (temperature exposure, C-rate, cycling depth) to predict impending stress early.
  • Transparent SHAP Explanations: Quantifies the exact mathematical contribution of high temperatures, harsh acceleration, or fast DC charging, converting predictions into clear preventive guidance.
  • Interactive What-If Simulation: Empowers drivers to evaluate alternative routes, charging cutoff thresholds (80% vs 100%), and driving modes before taking physical action.

Core Project Objectives

SparkEV is engineered around five foundational research objectives spanning edge sensing, predictive intelligence, and decentralized learning.

1. Personalized Driving Guidance

Analyze micro-driving telemetry (throttle jerk, brake pressure, speed variance) against individual driver baselines to deliver tailored eco-driving nudges that prevent unnecessary power spikes.

Module 1 · Rithanya K A

2. Contextual Charging Recommender

Formulate charging as a multi-criteria optimization problem balancing queue latency, tariff dynamics, route detour penalty, and battery longevity by preventing unnecessary 100% saturation.

Module 2 · Irene Divya J

3. Battery Health & SHAP XAI

Develop a multi-step temporal SOH degradation model incorporating cycling lag features, paired with TreeSHAP feature attributions to provide interpretable explanations for predicted risk tiers.

Module 3 · Shravanthi S

4. Privacy-First FedPer Architecture

Implement Federated Personalization (FedPer) splitting networks into a globally aggregated base representation ($\theta_{base}$) and localized personal classification heads ($\phi^k$), protecting user data.

Federated Learning · Gayathri U

5. Hardware Telemetry & What-If

Construct an OBD-II / CAN bus edge sensor acquisition pipeline with ESP32 microcontrollers alongside an interactive What-If scenario sandbox for testing alternative charging and driving decisions.

Hardware & Sim · Saravanapriyaa C R

6. Unified Interactive Documentation

Deliver an interactive web portal featuring architecture exploration, live parametric simulations, detailed API references, and open-source modular code for researchers and developers.

Project Progress & Roadmap

Tracking the iterative engineering, experimental validation, and paper publication trajectory of SparkEV.

Phase 1 · Completed Months 1–2

Literature Survey, Problem Formulation & Dataset Curation

Reviewed existing eco-driving, SOH estimation, and federated learning papers. Curated NASA PCoE battery aging datasets, Oxford battery degradation dataset, and recorded real-world EV OBD-II drive cycle traces.

Phase 2 · Completed Months 3–4

Hardware Sensing Prototype & CAN Telemetry Gateway

Constructed ESP32 + MCP2515 CAN transceiver hardware interface. Successfully decoded standard OBD-II PIDs (SoC, pack voltage, current, motor RPM, pedal position) and validated telemetry streaming via MQTT/WebSocket.

Phase 3 · Completed Months 5–6

Module Model Training & FedPer Federated Architecture

Implemented individual modules: Driving behavior classification (Mod 1), Multi-factor charging recommender (Mod 2), and Temporal SOH health regression (Mod 3). Deployed the FedPer split-model federated learning server in Flower.

Phase 4 · Current Focus Month 7 (In Progress)

SHAP Explainability, What-If Simulator & Portal Documentation

Integrated TreeSHAP explainability engine for battery stress factors. Built the interactive What-If scenario sandbox and compiled comprehensive web documentation deliverables.

Phase 5 · Upcoming Months 8–9

On-Road Vehicle Fleet Validation & Camera-Ready Publication

Deploy SparkEV edge daemon onto test electric vehicles at Amrita campus. Conduct comparative benchmarks against centralized models, finalize Table 1 experimental numbers, and submit paper to IEEE/Springer transactions.
